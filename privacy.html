# Privacy Policy — Nik5W1H Private **Effective date: 30 September 2026** Nik5W1H Private is a personal information and memory management application intended for use by the developer and a limited group of authorized users. This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when using Nik5W1H Private. ## 1. Storage Options Nik5W1H Private allows the user to select one of the following storage methods: **Local Storage** Information is stored locally on the user's device. **iCloud Storage** Information is stored and synchronized using Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services associated with the user's Apple Account. **Private Backend Storage** Authorized users may optionally store information on a privately operated Nik5W1H backend server. The private backend is not a general public storage service. Access is intended only for users authorized by the developer. ## 2. Information Stored by Nik5W1H Private Users decide what information they enter into Nik5W1H Private. This information may include: - titles and descriptions; - What, Where, When, Who, Why and How information; - notes; - photographs and images; - scanned documents and other files; - text extracted from images or documents using OCR; - location information; and - other attachments deliberately selected or created by the user. Nik5W1H Private does not require users to provide this information for advertising, profiling or marketing purposes. ## 3. Private Backend When Private Backend Storage is selected, information that the user chooses to save is transmitted to and stored on the privately operated Nik5W1H backend server. This may include text entered into memory items, photographs, documents, OCR text, saved location information and other attachments. Information stored on the private backend is used only to provide Nik5W1H functionality, including storing, retrieving, synchronizing, backing up and managing the user's Nik5W1H information. Information stored on the private backend is not sold to advertisers or data brokers and is not used for advertising, marketing or cross-app tracking. ## 4. Camera and Photo Library Nik5W1H Private may request access to the device camera or photo library when the user chooses to photograph, scan, select or attach an image or document. The app accesses these resources only when required for a feature selected by the user. When Private Backend Storage is selected, an image or document that the user chooses to save may be transmitted to and stored on the private backend. ## 5. Microphone and Speech Recognition Nik5W1H Private provides speech-to-text functionality. When the user chooses to use speech input, the app may access the microphone and Apple's speech-recognition technologies. Speech processing may occur on the device or through Apple services depending on the device, operating system, language and Apple's implementation. Recognized text may subsequently be stored using the storage option selected by the user. Nik5W1H Private does not operate its own speech-recognition service. ## 6. Location Information Nik5W1H Private may request access to the device's location when the user chooses to add a location to a memory item. Location information is used only for functionality requested by the user. When Private Backend Storage is selected, location information that the user deliberately saves may be transmitted to and stored on the private backend. Nik5W1H Private does not use location information for advertising or cross-app tracking and does not continuously monitor users' movements for advertising or profiling purposes. ## 7. Files, Documents and OCR Users may choose files or documents to attach to Nik5W1H items. Nik5W1H Private may also use device technologies to extract text from selected images or scanned documents using optical character recognition (OCR). Files, documents, images and OCR results are stored according to the storage option selected by the user: Local Storage, iCloud Storage or Private Backend Storage. ## 8. Apple Intelligence and Apple Services On supported Apple devices, Nik5W1H Private may use Apple-provided intelligence and other system technologies. Availability and processing are determined by the user's device, operating system and Apple services. Nik5W1H Private does not send information to a separate developer-operated artificial-intelligence service. ## 9. Advertising, Analytics and Tracking Nik5W1H Private does not use stored user information for advertising. Nik5W1H Private does not sell user information. Nik5W1H Private does not use stored user information to track users across applications or websites owned by other companies. The private backend does not provide stored Nik5W1H content to advertising networks or data brokers. ## 10. Sharing and Third-Party Services The developer does not intentionally share information stored on the private backend with advertisers, data brokers or unrelated third parties. Information may be processed by Apple when the user chooses features that rely on Apple services, including iCloud, CloudKit, speech recognition, location services, photo services and other operating-system functionality. Apple's handling of information is governed by Apple's applicable privacy policies and terms. Users may also voluntarily export, copy or share their own Nik5W1H information. Information shared at the user's direction is under the user's control. ## 11. Data Retention and Deletion Information stored locally remains on the user's device until it is deleted by the user or removed through application or device-management functions. Information stored using iCloud is managed through the user's Apple Account and Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services. Information stored on the private backend is retained while required to provide the Nik5W1H Private service or until it is deleted by the user or backend administrator. Nik5W1H Private provides functions for deleting individual items and associated information. An authorized user who wants information removed from the private backend may delete it through the app where the applicable deletion function is available or contact the developer to request deletion. Backend backups may retain deleted information temporarily until the relevant backup is replaced or expires as part of the normal backup-retention process. ## 12. Security Reasonable technical and administrative measures are used to restrict access to the private backend. Local and iCloud storage also rely on security mechanisms provided by Apple and the user's device and Apple Account. Users are responsible for protecting access to their devices, Apple Accounts and any credentials provided for access to the private backend. No electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. ## 13. User Choice and Consent Use of Private Backend Storage is optional. Users may instead select Local Storage or iCloud Storage. Users can revoke permissions for protected device resources such as the camera, microphone, photo library, speech recognition and location through the privacy settings provided by Apple. Users who no longer wish their information to be stored on the private backend may delete applicable information through Nik5W1H Private or contact the developer to request deletion. ## 14. Limited Distribution Nik5W1H Private is intended for the developer and a limited group of authorized users. Availability of the application does not by itself grant authorization to use the private backend. ## 15. Changes to This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated if Nik5W1H Private's functionality, storage methods or privacy practices change. The effective date at the top of this policy will be updated when material changes are made. ## 16.
