Privacy Policy — Nik5W1H Private

Effective Date: 30 September 2026

Nik5W1H Private is a personal memory and information management application. It allows users to record and organize information about belongings, people, documents, procedures and other matters using fields such as What, Where, When, Who, Why and How, together with notes and attachments.

Nik5W1H Private provides Local Storage, iCloud Storage and an optional capability to connect to a user-operated private backend.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled by the application.

1. Storage Options

Nik5W1H Private allows users to choose from the following storage options.

Local Storage

When Local Storage is selected, Nik5W1H information is stored locally on the user's device.

iCloud Storage

When iCloud Storage is selected, Nik5W1H information is stored and synchronized using Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services associated with the user's Apple Account.

Use of iCloud is subject to Apple's applicable terms and privacy policies.

Private Backend Storage

Nik5W1H Private includes an optional feature that allows users to connect the application to a compatible private backend storage service.

Important: The developer does not provide or operate a public backend storage service for Nik5W1H Private.

Users who choose Private Backend Storage are responsible for providing, configuring, operating, maintaining, backing up and securing their own compatible backend service and the infrastructure on which it operates.

The operator of a private backend is responsible for controlling access to that backend and for determining appropriate security, backup, retention and deletion practices.

Nik5W1H Private provides application functionality for communicating with a compatible backend. The developer does not undertake to provide backend hosting, administration, maintenance or technical support for independently operated backend services.

Private Backend Storage is optional. Users who do not operate a compatible private backend may use Local Storage or iCloud Storage instead.

2. Information Stored by Nik5W1H Private

Users decide what information they enter into Nik5W1H Private.

Depending on how the application is used, this information may include:

  • titles and descriptions;
  • information entered into What, Where, When, Who, Why and How fields;
  • notes;
  • photographs and images;
  • scanned documents;
  • files and other attachments;
  • text extracted from images or documents using optical character recognition (OCR);
  • location information; and
  • other information deliberately entered, selected or created by the user.

Nik5W1H Private is a general-purpose information management application. Users are responsible for deciding what information is appropriate to store in the application.

3. Information Sent to a Private Backend

When Private Backend Storage is selected, information that the user chooses to save may be transmitted from Nik5W1H Private to the backend configured by that user.

This may include:

  • memory-item text and notes;
  • photographs and images;
  • scanned documents and files;
  • OCR text;
  • saved location information; and
  • other attachments.

Nik5W1H Private uses this connection for functions such as creating, retrieving, updating and deleting information and attachments on the configured backend.

The developer does not use information stored on independently operated private backends for advertising, marketing or cross-app tracking.

The privacy, security and data-management practices applicable to an independently operated backend are the responsibility of its operator.

4. Camera and Photo Library

Nik5W1H Private may request access to the device camera or photo library when the user chooses to photograph, scan, select or attach an image or document.

Access occurs only when required for functionality initiated by the user and after any applicable system permission has been granted.

If the user chooses Private Backend Storage, photographs or images that the user elects to save may be transmitted to the configured private backend.

5. Microphone and Speech Recognition

Nik5W1H Private provides speech-to-text functionality.

When the user chooses to use speech input, the application may access the microphone and Apple's speech-recognition technologies. Speech processing may occur on the device or using Apple services depending on the device, operating system, language and Apple's implementation.

Recognized text may subsequently be stored using the storage option selected by the user.

Nik5W1H Private does not operate a separate developer-provided speech-recognition service.

6. Location

Nik5W1H Private may request access to the device's location when the user chooses to add location information to an item.

Location access is used for functionality initiated by the user.

If Private Backend Storage is selected, location information that the user chooses to save may be transmitted to and stored on the configured private backend.

Nik5W1H Private does not use location information for advertising or cross-app tracking.

The application is not designed to continuously monitor a user's movements for advertising or behavioural profiling.

7. Files, Documents and OCR

Users may choose files or documents to attach to Nik5W1H items.

Nik5W1H Private may also use device technologies to extract text from images or scanned documents using optical character recognition (OCR).

Files, documents, images and OCR results are stored according to the storage option selected by the user.

When Private Backend Storage is selected, such information may be transmitted to the configured private backend.

8. Apple Intelligence and Apple Services

On supported Apple devices, Nik5W1H Private may use Apple-provided intelligence and other operating-system technologies.

Availability and processing depend on the capabilities of the user's device, operating system and applicable Apple services.

Nik5W1H Private does not provide a separate developer-operated artificial-intelligence processing service.

Information processed by Apple services is subject to Apple's applicable privacy policies and terms.

9. Advertising and Tracking

Nik5W1H Private does not use information entered into the application for advertising.

The developer does not sell information entered into Nik5W1H Private to advertisers or data brokers.

Nik5W1H Private does not use information entered into the application to track users across applications or websites owned by other companies.

Nik5W1H Private does not include an advertising service based on the user's stored Nik5W1H content.

10. Sharing of Information

The developer does not intentionally provide user-created Nik5W1H content to advertisers, data brokers or unrelated third parties.

Information may be processed by Apple when the user chooses functionality that relies on Apple services, including iCloud, CloudKit, speech recognition, location services, photo services and other operating-system features.

Users may voluntarily export, copy, back up or share their own Nik5W1H information. Information exported or shared at the user's direction is under the user's control.

When a user configures Nik5W1H Private to communicate with an independently operated backend, information sent to that backend is subject to the practices and controls established by the backend operator.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Local Storage

Locally stored information remains on the user's device until it is deleted by the user or removed through application or device-management functions.

iCloud Storage

Information stored using iCloud is managed through the user's Apple Account and Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services. Apple's applicable retention and deletion practices apply.

Private Backend Storage

Retention and deletion of information stored on an independently operated private backend are determined by the operator of that backend.

Nik5W1H Private provides application functions for deleting applicable items and information from the configured backend.

A backend operator may maintain backups or other copies independently of Nik5W1H Private. Deleting an item through the application may therefore not necessarily remove copies retained in independently managed backup systems.

Users should contact the operator of their private backend regarding permanent deletion, backup retention or other backend data-management matters.

12. Security

Nik5W1H Private relies on security mechanisms provided by the operating system and device for locally stored information and on Apple's infrastructure when iCloud Storage is used.

Users choosing Private Backend Storage are responsible for implementing and maintaining appropriate security for their backend, including access controls, network security, software maintenance, backups and protection of any credentials used to access the service.

The developer does not warrant the security or availability of independently operated backend infrastructure.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. User Choice and Permissions

Private Backend Storage is optional. Users may choose Local Storage or iCloud Storage instead.

Access to protected device resources such as the camera, microphone, photo library, speech recognition and location is subject to Apple's permission mechanisms.

Users can review or revoke these permissions through the privacy settings provided by the operating system.

The consequences of changing or revoking a permission depend on the feature concerned. Other parts of Nik5W1H Private may continue to operate without that permission.

14. Private Backend Responsibilities

Users who choose to operate a private backend are responsible for:

  • providing compatible backend infrastructure;
  • configuring and maintaining that infrastructure;
  • restricting access to authorized users;
  • securing the server and network environment;
  • maintaining appropriate backups;
  • determining data-retention and deletion practices; and
  • complying with any laws or obligations applicable to information they choose to store.

The private backend capability is provided as an optional feature of Nik5W1H Private.

The developer does not provide a general-purpose Nik5W1H backend hosting service and does not undertake to provide technical support, administration, maintenance, security management or recovery services for independently operated backend systems.

15. Limited Distribution

Nik5W1H Private is intended for a limited group of users.

Access to the application does not provide or guarantee access to a private backend. Backend access must be separately configured and authorized by the operator of that backend.

16. Children's Privacy

Nik5W1H Private is a general-purpose personal information management application and is not specifically designed or marketed as a service for collecting personal information from children.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the functionality, storage options or privacy practices of Nik5W1H Private change.

The effective date shown at the top of this policy will be updated when material changes are made.
