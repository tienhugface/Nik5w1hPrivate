# Privacy Policy for Nik5W1H Private **Effective date: 30 September 2026** Nik5W1H is designed as a personal memory and information management application. It allows users to record information about belongings, people, documents, procedures, locations, dates, notes, and related attachments. This Privacy Policy applies to the publicly distributed version of Nik5W1H available through the Apple App Store. ## 1. Data Storage Nik5W1H does not operate a developer-controlled online service for users of the public App Store version. Information entered into Nik5W1H is stored using the storage option selected by the user: - **Local Storage:** Information is stored on the user's device. - **iCloud Storage:** Information is stored and synchronized using Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services associated with the user's Apple Account. The developer of Nik5W1H does not operate or control the user's iCloud account and does not receive a copy of the user's Nik5W1H data through a developer-operated server. The private backend storage system used by the developer for private versions of Nik5W1H is not available in the public App Store version. ## 2. Information Entered by the User Nik5W1H allows users to voluntarily enter and store information including: - titles and descriptions; - information about what, where, when, who, why, and how; - notes; - photographs and images; - scanned documents and other files; - OCR text extracted from images or documents; - location information; and - other attachments selected or created by the user. The content stored in Nik5W1H is determined entirely by the user. Users should avoid storing information about other people unless they have an appropriate reason or permission to do so. ## 3. Device Permissions Some Nik5W1H features may request access to Apple device services. ### Camera and Photos Nik5W1H may access the camera or photo library when the user chooses to photograph, scan, select, or attach an image or document. Access occurs only after the user grants the relevant system permission. ### Microphone and Speech Recognition Nik5W1H includes speech-to-text functionality. When the user chooses to use speech input, the app may access the microphone and Apple's speech-recognition services. Processing of speech may be performed by the device or Apple services depending on the device, operating system, language, and Apple's implementation. Nik5W1H does not send speech recordings to a developer-operated server. ### Location Nik5W1H may request access to location information when the user chooses to add their current location to a memory item. Location access is used for that user-requested feature and is not used for advertising, tracking, or monitoring the user's movements. ### Files and Documents The user may choose documents or files to attach to a Nik5W1H item. The app accesses files selected by the user for this purpose. ## 4. Apple Intelligence and On-Device Features On supported Apple devices, Nik5W1H may provide features using Apple-provided intelligence or system technologies. Availability and processing depend on the capabilities and privacy practices of the user's Apple device and Apple services. Nik5W1H does not send information used by these features to a developer-operated AI server in the public App Store version. ## 5. Tracking, Advertising and Analytics Nik5W1H does not use user information for advertising. Nik5W1H does not sell user information. Nik5W1H does not use user information to track users across apps or websites owned by other companies. Nik5W1H does not include a developer-operated advertising or behavioural profiling service. ## 6. Sharing of Information The developer does not sell or disclose Nik5W1H user-created content to advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties. Information may be processed by Apple when users choose Apple services such as iCloud, CloudKit, speech recognition, location services, photo services, or other operating-system features. Apple's handling of information is governed by Apple's applicable privacy policies and terms. Users may also voluntarily export, back up, copy, or share information from the app. Information shared or exported at the user's direction is under the user's control. ## 7. Data Retention and Deletion For Local Storage, information remains on the user's device until it is deleted by the user, erased using features provided by Nik5W1H, or removed as part of deleting or resetting application data. For iCloud Storage, information may remain in the user's iCloud account and synchronize between the user's devices according to Apple's iCloud and CloudKit operation and retention practices. Nik5W1H provides features for users to delete individual stored items and associated information. Because the developer does not maintain accounts or a developer-operated storage service for the public App Store version, the developer does not have access to remotely retrieve or delete a user's locally stored or private iCloud data. Users control their device and iCloud information through Nik5W1H and the controls provided by Apple. ## 8. Data Security Nik5W1H relies on Apple's operating-system security mechanisms and, when iCloud storage is selected, Apple's iCloud and CloudKit infrastructure. Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their devices and Apple Accounts, including appropriate device passcodes and Apple Account security. No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. ## 9. Children's Privacy Nik5W1H is a general-purpose personal information management application and is not specifically designed or marketed as a service for collecting personal information from children. The developer does not knowingly operate a server that collects personal information from children through the public App Store version of Nik5W1H. ## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated if Nik5W1H's functionality, storage methods, or privacy practices change. The effective date shown at the top of this policy will be updated when material changes are made. ## 11.
